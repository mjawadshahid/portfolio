## Why the LinkedIn post is a link and not an embed

You asked for LinkedIn posts embedded. Three constraints made that a bad idea
as the primary content:

- LinkedIn has no public API for listing your own posts. The Community
  Management API needs partner approval that personal sites don't get, so
  nothing here can auto-update, entries are curated by you.
- The official embed is a cross-origin iframe. Google cannot read a word of it,
  so embedding your best proof-of-work makes it invisible to search.
- It's heavy, it sets third-party cookies, and it breaks silently if a post's
  visibility changes.

So the talk lives here as your own words and your own photos, with `Event` and
`ImageObject` structured data attached. The `linkedin` field renders a
click-to-load facade underneath: nobody pays the loading cost unless they
actually want the discussion thread.
