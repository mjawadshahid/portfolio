Clinicians spend a large share of their day writing things down. Checkup
reports in particular are highly structured, highly repetitive, and almost
entirely derived from information that already exists somewhere in the record.

## What I built

An AI layer that drafts those reports from clinical inputs, and assistive
tooling for doctors alongside it. The design constraint that shaped everything:
the model produces a draft, and a clinician remains accountable for what is
signed. That rules out a lot of otherwise attractive architecture — anything
that obscures where a claim came from, or that makes editing harder than
writing from scratch.

Underneath sits the patient management system these organisations use to run
day-to-day operations.
